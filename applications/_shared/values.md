# Personal Thesis on Roles and Companies

What I look for. Used to filter opportunities and shape narrative in applications.

## Career profile in one sentence

Senior full-stack software engineer with 10+ years of industry experience shipping production software, holding a PhD in Computational Modeling and active affiliation with CERN's ATLAS Collaboration as a full author. Currently at Siteimprove (via Prime IT) and co-founder of Orkast.

## What I look for in a role

- **Engineering that compounds**: long-lived systems, well-designed code, public infrastructure over disposable throwaway work
- **Full-stack scope**: end-to-end ownership, or clear specialization at Staff-level depth
- **AI-adjacent**: roles at the intersection of full-stack engineering and applied AI (integration, tooling, or product features), aligned with how I already work daily at Orkast with Claude Code
- **Modern stack overlap**: Node.js, TypeScript, React are my daily. Java (Spring Boot) via Siteimprove is a strong secondary. Python is my scientific/computing daily.
- **Open-source proximity**: companies that build in the open, contribute upstream, or run on open-source foundations

## What I look for in a company

- **Mission-driven**, not just profitable
- **Profitable and independent** over VC-hooked and churny
- **Small teams with high talent density** over large teams with lower bar
- **International, diverse, English-working environment**
- **Engineering culture that respects rigor** — quality, testing, code review, architecture thinking

## Geographic preference

- **Primary target**: Geneva, Switzerland (and Romandie / French-Swiss region)
- **Also acceptable**: London, Zurich, other Swiss cantons
- **Remote from Brazil**: only if the compensation is exceptional and the role is truly remote-friendly

## What I explicitly do NOT want

- Pure marketing/growth engineering (unless attached to a mission I care about)
- VC-hooked churn factories
- Roles that would require me to give up open-source contribution
- Positions that ignore the scientific software track (that's a fit signal for me)
